waving_hand Welcome to AvidTune

AvidTune is a modern, free, and open-source Android music streaming app designed to deliver a premium listening experience. Built with Material Design 3, it offers ad-free streaming directly from YouTube Music, deeply customizable aesthetics, and native offline playback.

What is AvidTune?

Developed by cgens67, AvidTune serves as an alternative YouTube Music client focused on performance, design, and privacy. Unlike official clients, AvidTune completely removes tracking, telemetry, and advertisements.

Key Features at a Glance

  • Ad-Free Playback: Enjoy uninterrupted music without visual or audio advertisements.
  • Offline Mode: Download tracks and playlists for listening without an internet connection.
  • Synchronized Lyrics: Real-time lyrics that flow with your music.
  • Material Design 3: Fluid animations, dynamic colors, and customizable themes.
  • YouTube Integration: Sign in securely to sync your playlists and liked songs.
  • Privacy-First: No trackers, analytics, or background data collection.

download_for_offline Chapter 1 — Installation

This section walks you through getting AvidTune onto your Android device safely and correctly.

System Requirements

  • OS: Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher.
  • Storage: ~35 MB for the base app. Recommended 1GB+ if utilizing offline caching and downloads.
  • Network: Internet connection required for streaming and initial login.

Installing AvidTune

Because AvidTune is a third-party YouTube client, it is not available on the Google Play Store. You must install the APK manually.

download Install AvidTune
  1. Visit the GitHub Releases page (or use the button above to grab the latest stable APK).
  2. Download the latest .apk file (usually named app-universal-release.apk).
  3. Open the file on your device. You may be prompted to allow "Install from unknown sources" in your Android security settings.
  4. Tap Install and wait for the process to complete.
warning
Security Warning

Always download either in the official website of AvidTune (avidtune.vercel.app), or the official github repo.

First Launch & Permissions

On your first launch, AvidTune will request basic permissions to function optimally:

  • Notifications: Required to show the media playback controls in your notification shade and lock screen.
  • Storage/Media: Required if you wish to export backups or save downloaded music to public directories.

Updating AvidTune

AvidTune includes an in-app updater. Go to Settings, and it will say "New version available!" if there's an update. Simply tap it to download the latest release.

tune Chapter 2 — Setting Up

Customize AvidTune to match your preferences and synchronize your data.

Account & Sync

You can use AvidTune entirely anonymously. However, logging in allows you to access your YouTube Music library.

  • Navigate to Settings > Account and sign in.
  • Once authenticated, your Playlists, Liked Songs, and Recommendations will automatically populate in the Library tab.

Player & Audio Quality

AvidTune streams high-quality audio directly from YouTube's servers. Under Settings > Player and audio, you can set the default audio quality to prioritize data saving or maximum fidelity.

  • Skip Silence: Automatically jumps over silent gaps at the beginning and end of tracks.
  • Audio Normalization: Keeps volume consistent across varying albums and eras.

Appearance & Theming

In Settings > Appearance, you can force a specific color palette or force Dark/Light mode to perfectly match your aesthetic.

Privacy & Storage

Manage your footprint locally. In Settings > Privacy, you can clear your search history and listening cache.

In Settings > Storage, you can define exactly how much megabytes/gigabytes of space AvidTune is allowed to use for caching images and streaming audio. You can also wipe all offline downloads here with one tap.

handyman Chapter 3 — Troubleshooting

Playback Issues

Music won't play or buffers endlessly:

  • Verify your internet connection.
  • YouTube occasionally rotates their API structures. If playback breaks globally, check GitHub for a new update.
  • Try clearing the app cache via Android Settings.

App Crashes

If the app crashes consistently on launch:

  1. Go to Android Settings > Apps > AvidTune.
  2. Tap "Storage & Cache".
  3. Tap "Clear Data" (Note: This will log you out and remove local settings).

Still need help?

The best way to get support or report a bug is by opening an issue on our GitHub tracker. Be sure to include your Android version and the exact steps to reproduce the issue.

More Than Just a Project

AvidTune is a free and open-source music player for Android that brings together the best features from modern music apps. Built with premium design principles, it offers a beautiful, personalized experience that adapts to your style.

The project is powered by a community of contributors who believe music should be accessible to everyone, without ads and subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AvidTune? expand_more
AvidTune is a modern, free, and open-source YouTube Music client for Android. It focuses on a beautifully crafted Material Design 3 experience while retaining the core features needed for effortless music streaming.
Is AvidTune free and ad-free? expand_more
Yes, AvidTune is completely free, licensed under GPL-3.0, and contains zero ads.
Does it support offline playback? expand_more
Absolutely. You can download and cache your favorite tracks to listen offline seamlessly.
How do I install or update? expand_more
You can download the APK file directly from our website by using the download buttons, or directly from the official GitHub Releases page. Install it manually like any standard Android APK.

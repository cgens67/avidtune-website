Frequently Asked Questions

What is AvidTune? expand_more
AvidTune is a free and open-source music player for Android. It streams high-fidelity music from YouTube Music completely ad-free and offers an intuitive Material Design 3 interface that adapts to your style.
Is AvidTune free and ad-free? expand_more
Yes, AvidTune is 100% free and open-source under the GPL-3.0 license. It contains no ads, paid subscriptions, or tracking.
Does it support offline playback? expand_more
Yes! You can download and cache your favorite tracks to listen offline. The storage system organizes files neatly on your internal storage.
Is my listening data private? expand_more
Yes. AvidTune respects your privacy with zero external tracking. Your playlists, listening stats, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
How do I install or update AvidTune? expand_more
You can download the APK directly using the download buttons on this site or from our GitHub Releases page. Simply install the APK file on your Android device (Android 8.0+).

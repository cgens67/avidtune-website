Features

Offline downloads

Cache songs for offline playback with configurable quality and storage management.

Live lyrics

Follow along with synchronized lyrics in the player and lock screen widgets.

Themeable UI

Light, dark, black and dynamic themes built on Material 3 design.

Highly Customizable Discord Rich Presence

Tailor exactly how your presence appears in Discord, with detailed fields, artwork selection, timestamps and flexible formatting options so you can show what matters.

Smart RPC Translation

Automatically translate your Rich Presence text before it’s sent to Discord — making your activity accessible across languages and audiences.

Lyrics Translation Support

Translate lyrics directly inside ArchiveTune so listeners can follow along in their preferred language.
